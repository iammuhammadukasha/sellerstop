To show the Seller Stop Choice logo in the navbar and favicon:

1. Save your logo image (the one with the house + $ and "ellerstopchoice" text) as:
   logo.png

2. Place it in this folder (cash-buyer-landing/public/).

3. Restart or refresh the dev server. The navbar and browser tab will then show your logo.

If logo.png is missing, the navbar shows "Seller Stop Choice" as text instead.
